About Adom

Ten students in 2020. Seven courses now.

Adom Technologies started in 2019 in Mpumalanga, the Province of the Rising Sun. We build software and fix tech for local businesses, and we teach people to do the same.

How we got here

2019 Adom opens A software and support company at Lindokuhle House, Nelspruit.
2020 First 10 students One programme: the Diploma in Information Technology.
Now 7 courses, 801 projects Programming, technology, design and business, taught next to live client work.
Mission

Teach what employers are asking for, to a high standard, and keep improving it every intake.

Vision

Technology without limit. Graduates who are ready for the jobs automation is creating.
